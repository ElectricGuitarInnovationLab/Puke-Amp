# Puke Amp Trademark and Branding Guidelines

## Official project

Puke Amp is the guitar amplifier modeling plugin and asset library maintained
by the RATLab as part of Puke Studio. The official project includes the canonical
repository and the releases and documentation published by its maintainers.
[Puke Amp repository](https://github.com/ElectricGuitarInnovationLab/Puke-Amp)

The [software license](LICENSE) governs the code. The [asset license](ASSETS_LICENSE.md)
governs the covered audio assets. These guidelines address the Puke Amp name,
logo, and other Puke Studio branding used to identify the official project.

## Names, forks, and derivative works

You may use the name Puke Amp to refer to the official project, describe
compatibility, or identify the source of a fork or derivative work. Give modified
distributions a distinct name and make their origin clear. For example,
“Example Amp (based on Puke Amp)” identifies the relationship, while
“Official Puke Amp Extended” implies official status.

Avoid names, logos, and other branding that could mislead users about who
maintains a distribution or whether the RATLab or Puke Studio endorses it.
Official logos may be used to identify the official project. Use an original
visual identity when branding a derivative distribution.

## Commercial use and questions

The applicable software and asset licenses govern commercial use. The same
branding guidelines apply to commercial distributions. Contact the maintainers
with questions about branding, commercial licensing, or collaboration.

Puke Amp derives in part from Neural Amp Modeler, and the included captures
refer to equipment by other manufacturers. See the
[third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY.md) and the README's
[equipment trademark notice](README.md#trademark-notice).
